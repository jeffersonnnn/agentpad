# Available worldwide, no geoblock, including the stock-trading tier

---
status: accepted
---

The launchpad launches entertainment-first persona coins and offers a premium tier whose
agents trade tokenized stocks on Robinhood Chain. Robinhood stock tokens are tokenized
securities that bar US, UK, Canadian, and Swiss persons by their own terms. We decided to
serve the whole world with no region check, including the stock-trading tier, because the
founder wants global reach. The legal question is routed to the legal department as a
separate track, not resolved here.

## Considered options

- **Full-site geoblock of US/UK/CA/CH.** Rejected. It blocks the whole audience for a risk
  that only the trading tier carries.
- **Geoblock the trading tier only.** Rejected by the founder in favour of global reach.
- **No geoblock (chosen).** Maximum reach. Highest legal exposure.

## Consequences

- Restricted persons can reach tokenized securities through our agents. This may breach the
  stock-token terms and securities law.
- An agent that pools token-holder money and trades securities may be characterised as an
  unregistered fund or broker. That exposure lands on the operator.
- Mitigation is deferred to a legal review. Revisit this ADR after that review. If legal
  requires a gate, the tier-only geoblock is the ready fallback.
